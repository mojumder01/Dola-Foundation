What We Do

Our Programs

Dola Foundation runs six core programs designed to address the most pressing needs of underprivileged communities across Bangladesh.

Education

Providing quality education and scholarships to underprivileged children, ensuring every child has access to learning opportunities.

Learn more

Healthcare

Free medical camps and essential healthcare services for rural communities who lack access to basic medical care.

Learn more

Charity & Relief

Emergency relief, food distribution, and aid for disaster-affected families when they need it most.

Learn more

Environment

Tree planting, clean-up drives, and climate awareness initiatives to protect Bangladesh's precious natural environment.

Learn more

Youth Development

Leadership training, vocational skills, and mentorship programs empowering young people to build bright futures.

Learn more

Orphan Care

Shelter, education, and emotional support for orphaned children, providing them a safe and nurturing environment.

Learn more
