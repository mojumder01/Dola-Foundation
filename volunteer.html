Join Our Mission

Be the change you wish to see. Join 500+ volunteers transforming lives across Bangladesh — every skill you have can make a difference.

Why Volunteer

The Difference You Make

Volunteering with Dola Foundation is more than giving your time — it's a life-changing experience for you and the communities you serve.

Real Impact

Your work directly translates into meals delivered, children taught, patients treated, and disasters survived. You will see the change you create with your own eyes.

Grow Your Skills

Gain hands-on experience in community development, public health, education, logistics, and leadership. Our volunteer training programs are internationally recognized.

Join a Community

Become part of a passionate, diverse network of 500+ changemakers. Build lifelong friendships with people who share your values and vision for a better Bangladesh.

1

Apply Online

Fill out the volunteer registration form below. Tell us about your skills, availability, and which programs you are most passionate about. There is no application fee and no minimum commitment.

2

Brief Interview

A member of our volunteer coordination team will reach out within 3 business days to schedule a brief 20-minute conversation. We want to understand your goals and find the perfect fit for your skills.

3

Serve Your Community

Attend our half-day volunteer orientation, meet your team, receive your training materials, and begin making a difference. We provide full support and supervision throughout your journey with us.
